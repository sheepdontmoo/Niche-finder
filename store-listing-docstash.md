## Full description (4000 chars max)

**Scan documents to searchable PDF — the one-time price CamScanner used to be, before it wasn't.**

DocStash is a document scanner built for people done with scanner apps that charge once, then
quietly find a way to charge again. Point your camera at a document, DocStash finds the edges,
straightens the perspective, and turns it into a clean, searchable file — all on your phone, all
in the time it takes to say "cheese."

**📄 SCAN ANY DOCUMENT IN SECONDS**
Automatic edge detection and perspective correction on every page, powered by Google's own
on-device scanner engine — no fiddling with crop handles, no blurry corners, no ads before you
can even take the photo.

**🔎 SEARCH INSIDE YOUR OWN DOCUMENTS**
Every page gets a real, on-device text layer the moment you scan it. Type a word from any
receipt, contract, or notice into search and jump straight to the page — you're not just storing
photos of paper anymore.

**📚 A LIBRARY YOU CAN ACTUALLY FIND THINGS IN**
Every scan lands in one searchable library on your phone. Rename documents, add pages to an
existing scan later, delete what you don't need — no folders to manage, no clutter to fight.

**📤 EXPORT AS A REAL SEARCHABLE PDF, OR PLAIN IMAGES**
Combine every page into one searchable PDF, or export each page as a standalone image — your
choice, every time, with no watermark stamped across either one.

**🔒 YOUR DOCUMENTS STAY ON YOUR PHONE**
No account to create, no forced cloud upload, no "processing" your paperwork on someone else's
server. DocStash works fully offline once installed — the way scanning your own documents should
work.

**💳 ONE PRICE. PAID ONCE. THAT'S IT.**
Unlock DocStash Pro for a single one-time payment: combined multi-page searchable PDF export,
yours forever. No monthly fee, no yearly renewal, no surprise charge for a feature you already
paid for. No ads, no page limits, no watermarks — on the free tier or Pro.

Whether you're searching for a document scanner, a PDF scanner, a receipt scanner, an OCR app, or
just a scanner app that doesn't quietly turn into a subscription, DocStash is built to be the one
you keep.

  stores payment details
- Users can request deletion: nothing to delete on our end — there is no account and no server;
  uninstalling the app removes all local data immediately
