# /acq-sales — sales system

Working on:

> $ARGUMENTS

Read `knowledge/acq/05-sales.md` first. Read `knowledge/acq/01-offers.md` if the
problem might actually be the offer — a weak offer presents as a sales problem
and no amount of sales coaching fixes it.

**Check first:** is close rate the constraint, or is it show rate, lead quality,
or the offer? Diagnose before prescribing.

---

## Mode: Speed to lead (check this first, always)

Usually the highest-leverage fix available.

- **Target: contact within 60 seconds** of an application without a booking
- Conversion drops roughly **70% → 20%** if the lead leaves without booking
- Measure the operator's actual **median** speed to lead in seconds — not their
  impression of it
- Implement the free-for-all: unbooked applicants go open, first setter claims
  with a double dial + text, lead stays live 7 days if contacted daily
- Use a dummy internal calendar with extended windows so there's always a slot

---

## Mode: Rubric

Build an objective grading rubric of **~31 call components** for this specific
business and offer. For each: name, what "great" looks like, what "not quite"
looks like.

Operating rules:
- Score *not quite* / *good* / *great*
- **Feedback on the single lowest-scoring component only.** One correction per
  review — multiple corrections produce zero change
- Build a separate **off-call rubric**: health, mindset, CRM hygiene, admin
- Delegate grading to a QC VA; the manager's bandwidth is the real constraint on
  team improvement

---

## Mode: Script

Build against the Value Equation and the obstacle inventory. Every objection in
the script maps to an obstacle from `/acq-offer`.

**Operationalise every soft skill.** Never write "listen actively" — write the
checkable behaviours:
1. Head nodding while they speak
2. Verbal affirmations ("ah", "ooh", "mm")
3. Mirroring — reciting their information back in fewer words

If a script instruction can't be graded on a rubric, rewrite it until it can.

---

## Mode: Incentives

Every reward is amplified by delivering it **randomly, quickly, and publicly**.

- **Weekly competitions, not monthly** — keeps all seven days live, incentivises
  weekends, lets a bad week reset
- Tiered cash bonuses **plus** random non-cash rewards
- Additional comps: CRM accuracy, daily closing rate (cuts follow-up load),
  random pipeline-activity bonuses
- **Team-wide praise channel** — every new client celebrated by everyone,
  immediately. Free, continuous reinforcement of the most important behaviour

---

## Mode: Team structure

At **3+ closers or setters**, install the player-coach:
- Top closer → **Closer Lead** over 3+ reps
- Top setter → **Setter Lead** over 3+ reps
- **80% revenue-generating work / 20% coaching**
- **Comp = base role income + % of pod performance**

Keeps your best performer producing while multiplying their method, and aligns
their pay with pod output.

**Training method:** modeling then roleplay. The learner watches the top closer
take real calls, then roleplays with them. Reading a script is neither and
changes nothing.

---

## Rules
- Speed to lead before anything else — it's usually the biggest single number.
- One correction per review.
- If a behaviour can't be graded, it can't be trained — unbundle it.
- A weak offer presents as a sales problem. Check the offer before blaming reps.
