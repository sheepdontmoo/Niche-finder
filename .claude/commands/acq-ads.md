# /acq-ads — creative and conversion

Working on:

> $ARGUMENTS

Read `knowledge/acq/04-marketing-ops.md` first, and `knowledge/acq/01-offers.md`
for the offer being advertised. Read the matching `business-context/` file if one exists.

**Check first:** is conversion, not traffic, the actual constraint? Buying traffic
to feed a leaky funnel is the most expensive available mistake. If page-level
conversion data doesn't exist, getting it is the first deliverable.

---

## Mode: CRO

1. Map conversion at **every** funnel step (visit → opt-in → booked → showed → closed)
2. Identify the weakest step and **quantify the prize** ("35% → 50% opt-in = +42% business")
3. Check page load time — ~7% revenue lost per second of delay
4. Write the test: one meaningful test per week, targeting **~50% relative** improvement
5. Concentrate 80% of the work above the fold, desktop and mobile

Opt-in page structure: **headline → video/image → testimonials**, all above the
fold where possible. Use incomplete-information bias (blurred assets, locked
video, partial reveals) to lift opt-ins.

---

## Mode: Ads (assembly)

### Week 1
- Write **6 primary angles** — genuinely distinct arguments, not reworded hooks
- **5 hooks per angle → 30 ads**
- Each ad in **its own ad set**
- Spend enough per ad to reach a verdict. Underspending buys ambiguity, which is
  worse than a loss

### Week 2+ — 70/20/10
| Share | What |
|---|---|
| 70% | Slight variations of the top 2 performers |
| 20% | Moderate variations — different hooks, speakers, settings |
| 10% | Completely new angles |

The 10% is what prevents the cliff when the current winner fatigues. Cutting it
to fund the 70% is the most common and most expensive scaling error.

**Deliverable:** the actual 6 angles × 5 hooks written out, ready to film or ship.

---

## Mode: VSL / long-form copy

Structure against the **Value Equation** — every section should raise Dream
Outcome or Perceived Likelihood, or lower Time Delay or Effort:

1. **Hook** — the promise, stated as the dream outcome, in their words
2. **Problem** — the obstacle inventory from `/acq-offer`, named so they feel seen
3. **Mechanism** — why this works when what they've tried didn't (raises likelihood)
4. **Proof** — cases, numbers, specificity (raises likelihood)
5. **Offer** — the stack, with values (raises dream outcome)
6. **Risk reversal** — the guarantee (lowers effort/sacrifice)
7. **Scarcity/urgency** — real constraints only
8. **CTA** — one action, stated plainly and repeatedly

Write it in the operator's voice, for their avatar. Ask for a sample of their
existing copy or a transcript before writing, so voice matches.

---

## Rules
- Volume of tested angles beats polish of individual assets.
- Never scale a winning ad without a live 10% exploration budget.
- Quantify the prize before recommending any work.
- Fix load speed before buying more traffic — it's the cheapest win available.
- Real scarcity only. Fake deadlines train an audience to ignore deadlines.
