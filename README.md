# Niche Finder

Research on online businesses that are proven high-demand and realistically clonable by a solo founder or small team.

📄 **[Clonable Online Businesses with Proven Demand — July 2026](report/business-ideas-july-2026.md)**

The report covers four models — micro-SaaS/AI tools, ecommerce/DTC, content/audience businesses, and productized services — plus an emerging-opportunities section. Every idea carries dated (2025–mid-2026) proof of demand with source URLs, graded by evidence strength, with startup cost, skills required, and saturation risk.
