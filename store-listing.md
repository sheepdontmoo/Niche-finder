## Full description (4000 chars max)

**The yardage you need, the moment you glance.**

Fairway Caddie is a golf GPS rangefinder and scorecard that respects your round. No cluttered
maps, no pop-ups, no feed — just the number that matters, huge enough to read in full sunlight
with the club already in your hand.

**⛳ THE NUMBER YOU NEED**
Front, middle and back of the green from exactly where you're standing. The middle distance is
the biggest thing on screen — the way it should be.

**🟢 SEE THE ACTUAL GREEN**
Not an icon — the real mapped shape of the green you're playing, rotated to your approach line,
with the front edge marked. Know if the pin's tucked behind a narrow front before you choose
the club.

**✏️ SCORE IN ONE TAP**
Tap your score, move on — par is pre-highlighted, and the card advances to the next hole with
you. Classic scorecard notation: red circles for birdies, squares for bogeys, with to-par and
Stableford totals.

**📏 KNOW YOUR REAL CLUB DISTANCES**
Mark your ball, hit, walk — Fairway Caddie measures the shot. Finally settle what your 7-iron
actually does.

**🔋 BUILT FOR A FULL 18**
Screen stays awake, courses save to your phone for offline play, and auto-advance follows you
to the next tee. No signal on the back nine? No problem.

**🗺️ THOUSANDS OF COURSES**
Powered by OpenStreetMap's worldwide course data — and improving every week. Try your course
free before deciding anything.

**Free forever:** GPS distances, scorecard, demo mode.
**Caddie Plus (optional):** offline courses, shot measuring, club distances, round history —
simple yearly or monthly plan with a free trial. No ads either way.

Course data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
