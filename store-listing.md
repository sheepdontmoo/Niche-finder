## Full description (4000 chars max)

**Golf GPS rangefinder and scorecard — the yardage you need, the moment you glance.**

Fairway Caddie is a golf GPS app built for players who are done with cluttered rangefinder apps
and yearly subscriptions. No maps to squint at, no pop-ups, no feed — just accurate golf GPS
distances big enough to read in full sunlight, with the club already in your hand.

**⛳ GOLF GPS DISTANCES: FRONT, MIDDLE, BACK**
The core of any golf rangefinder app, done right. Front, middle and back of the green from
exactly where you're standing — the middle yardage is the biggest thing on screen, the way a
golf GPS should work.

**🟢 SEE THE ACTUAL GREEN, NOT AN ICON**
The real mapped shape of the green you're playing, rotated to your approach line, with the
front edge marked. Know if the pin's tucked behind a narrow front before you choose the club —
something no other golf GPS rangefinder shows you.

**✏️ GOLF SCORECARD: SCORE IN ONE TAP**
Tap your score, move on — par is pre-highlighted, and the scorecard advances to the next hole
with you. Classic notation: red circles for birdies, squares for bogeys, with to-par and
Stableford totals tracked automatically.

**📏 KNOW YOUR REAL CLUB DISTANCES**
Mark your ball, hit, walk — Fairway Caddie measures the shot. Finally settle what your 7-iron
actually does, round after round.

**🔋 OFFLINE GOLF GPS FOR A FULL 18**
Screen stays awake, courses save to your phone for offline play with no signal, and auto-advance
follows you to the next tee. Built for links golf and dead zones on the back nine.

**🗺️ THOUSANDS OF GOLF COURSES WORLDWIDE**
Powered by OpenStreetMap's course data — growing every week. Search your golf course and try it
free before deciding anything.

**💳 NO SUBSCRIPTION TRAP**
Free forever: golf GPS distances, digital scorecard, demo round.
Caddie Plus (optional): offline courses, shot distance measuring, club distances, round history
— a simple yearly or monthly plan with a free trial, cancel anytime. No ads, ever, on either tier.

Whether you're searching for a golf GPS app, a golf rangefinder, a digital golf scorecard, or
just a better alternative to your current golf GPS watch or app — Fairway Caddie is built to be
the one you keep.

Course data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
