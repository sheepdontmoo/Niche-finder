## Standing diagnosis (2026-08-09)

**Binding constraint: Leads** — no acquisition channel has ever been run for this
business. Not underperforming; never attempted.

Rungs above it are clean: conversion can't bind with a zero numerator, sales has
no leads to close, and the offer has already closed two international federations.

**The structural asset:** the market is *enumerable*. Roughly 35 Olympic IFs, ~40
further IOC-recognised IFs, ~350–400 continental confederations, plus NGBs with
real output volume — a tight ICP of perhaps **300–800 organisations worldwide**,
every one publicly listed and contactable. The entire market fits in a
spreadsheet and can be worked in a quarter by one person.

**Why paid ads are wrong here:** Meta cannot target "Secretary General of an
international federation." The account already shows the symptom — the SupaDesign
USA campaign paid EUR 33.17 for 327 impressions, a EUR 101 CPM, because the
audience was too narrow to deliver.

**Prescription, in order:**
1. Referral asks to WAKO and ITA. Federation leadership meets at SportAccord,
   ANOC and congresses; a referral-shaped ask costs nothing and outruns any ad.
2. Enumerate the TAM and run cold outreach — Clay and the `/linkedin` skill are
   already connected. Volume, personalisation, persistence, all three.
3. Park SupaDesign until 281Sport has ten clients. One offer, one avatar.

**Next constraint the fix will expose:** Sales, and the offer page behind it — no
price, no guarantee, no quantified outcome, CTA is "email Darren."
