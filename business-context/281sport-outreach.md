# 281Sport — outreach drafts

Drafted 2026-08-09. Step 1 of the prescription: the referral asks. These cost
nothing and, per `knowledge/acq/02-leads.md`, warm outreach is the fastest
zero-cost path to revenue and is *routinely skipped because it's uncomfortable*.

**Send these before building any list.**

---

## The craft point that decides whether these work

**Do not ask "who do you know?"** It's a cognitively expensive question. The
recipient has to generate names from nothing, so the honest answer is usually
"let me think about it," which means never.

**Name specific federations instead.** Then they only have to *react* — "yes,
talk to Anna at IFF, not the bowling lot." That is a two-second reply instead of
a ten-minute task, and it is the entire difference between a referral ask that
works and one that gets archived.

Second rule: **do not pitch.** The moment this reads as selling through them, it
costs you the relationship you're trading on. Ask for direction, not for a deal.

---

## 1. WAKO — the ARISF anchor

**Why WAKO first:** they are an ARISF member. That association has 40 federations
who meet at a General Assembly and at SportAccord. One reply here reaches deeper
than any list.

**To:** your main day-to-day contact — the person who actually sends you the work,
not the President.
**Subject:** Quick question — other federations

> Hi [Name],
>
> Nothing needed on [current/recent project] — that's all on track.
>
> A question instead, if you have a second. I'm looking to work with two or three
> more federations this year, and I'd rather be introduced than turn up cold.
>
> You'd know better than me who's actually struggling with output. I was thinking
> of the smaller ARISF members — [Federation A], [Federation B], [Federation C] —
> the ones without a full in-house comms team who are still expected to produce
> event graphics, sponsor decks and congress material on the same schedule as the
> big federations.
>
> Is there anyone in that group you'd point me at? Or anyone at the ARISF
> secretariat worth knowing?
>
> Happy to write the intro email myself so it's a forward and nothing more.
>
> Thanks,
> Darren

**Fill in A/B/C from `281sport-prospects.md`** — pick three plausibly in WAKO's
orbit. Good candidates: IFMA (Muaythai), World Karate Federation, International
Sambo Federation — adjacent combat sports, similar scale, likely already known to
them.

**Why this works:** it opens by removing any worry that something's wrong, asks
for direction rather than a favour, names three so they can react instead of
generate, and removes the last obstacle by offering to draft the intro.

## 2. ITA

**To:** your contact there
**Subject:** Quick question

> Hi [Name],
>
> [Current work] is in hand — no action needed.
>
> Separately: I'm taking on a couple more federations this year and would rather
> come recommended than cold. Given how many governing bodies you deal with,
> you'll have a much better sense than I do of who's under-resourced on the
> communications side.
>
> Anyone you'd suggest I speak to? Happy to draft the intro so it's just a
> forward for you.
>
> Thanks,
> Darren

**Note:** ITA works across a very wide range of governing bodies, so their view of
who is under-resourced is unusually broad. Worth the ask even though the
relationship may be narrower than WAKO's.

## 3. If a referral lands — the follow-up

Do not send a brochure. Lead with evidence you've looked at *their* output:

> Hi [Name],
>
> [Referrer] suggested I get in touch.
>
> I run the design and production queue for WAKO — event graphics, sponsor decks,
> member comms, congress material — as a monthly retainer, so it comes off the
> secretariat's plate entirely.
>
> I had a look at your [specific recent event/championship] material. [One
> specific, accurate, non-insulting observation.]
>
> Worth a short call to see if the same setup would help? Either way, happy to
> send over what we produce for WAKO so you can see the standard.
>
> Darren

**The specific observation is mandatory.** Without it this is a template and reads
like one. With it, you are visibly the only person who bothered to look.

---

## Sequencing note

Send the two referral asks **first**, and wait. If WAKO points you at three
federations, those three are worth more than the other 68 combined, and the cold
sequence should be built around whatever they tell you — including which
federations *not* to bother with.

Building the full list first, then asking, wastes the most valuable input you can
get.
