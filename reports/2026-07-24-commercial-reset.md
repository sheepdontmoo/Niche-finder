## THE VERDICT FIRST

**The winner is: a recurring "appointment-leak recovery" service for private clinics in Ireland — dentists, physios and aesthetics clinics — sold under SupaDesign, opened with a mystery-call audit of the clinic's own front desk, piloted at €990 for 30 days, continuing at €495–€995/month.**

Why this and not something else:

1. **These buyers verifiably have money and verifiably leak it.** UK clinic owners already spend £400–£2,000+/month on marketing (HMDG Private Practice Barometer, n=715 owners, 2025 — VERIFIED). A single practice loses ~£56,000/year to no-shows (BDA-reported — VERIFIED). The Irish government's own RCT proved SMS reminders cut missed appointments 13% at €129 each (Dept of Health — VERIFIED). One dental implant in Ireland is €2,000–€3,500; Invisalign is €2,500–€6,500 (published clinic price lists — VERIFIED). One recovered case pays for months of Darren's service.
2. **Nobody owns the leak.** Marketing agencies stop at the lead. Front desks miss 28–38% of calls (US-verified; no UK/IE study exists — which means Darren's own audit data becomes a proprietary sales asset). The gap between "paid enquiry" and "attended appointment" is the best-evidenced, least-served spend cluster found in this entire investigation.
3. **It requires zero building.** Delivery is off-the-shelf tools plus Darren's hands: missed-call text-back, 5-minute follow-up, reminder/deposit workflow, review engine, a weekly one-page leak report. The sequence is sell → deliver manually → automate later.
4. **It matches SupaDesign as it exists today.** The live site already names clinics, dentists and physios as targets, already prices at €495/€995, already promises async no-meetings delivery. No repositioning is needed before validation — only a narrower conversation.
5. **It directly counters the failure pattern.** The €900 coach offer failed against buyers with thin wallets. A dentist selling €3,000 implants who loses one in seven booked patients has both the money and the measurable pain. This is a market-selection fix, not an effort fix.

The full reasoning, the landscape, the ranking, the offer and the seven-day attack follow. Two credible runners-up (renewable-energy installers' quote-speed, and productised invoice-chasing for Irish SMEs) are deep-dived so the choice is transparent — but the recommendation is singular.

## 4. DEEP-DIVE: TOP THREE

### 4A. Clinic Appointment-Leak Recovery (WINNER)

- **Problem in the buyer's language:** "I'm paying for marketing and the phone still rings out at lunch. Patients book and don't turn up. I only find out at the end of the month when the numbers are down."
- **ICP:** Private/mixed dental practices, physio/chiro clinics, aesthetics clinics in Ireland (start Cork/Munster). 2–15 staff, treatment value ≥€60/visit, visible marketing activity (running Meta/Google ads, or an active GBP with 50+ reviews). Sweet spot: dental with high-value elective lines (implants, Invisalign) and multi-practitioner physio.
- **Decision-maker:** Principal dentist / owner-clinician / practice manager (dental); owner-physio (physio); founder (aesthetics). Identifiable by name from clinic websites and registers.
- **Buying trigger:** Ad spend up + diary flat; a no-show-heavy week; receptionist departure; a competitor visibly outranking them on Google.
- **Existing alternatives & spend:** marketing agencies £400–£2,000+/mo (generate leads, don't answer phones); PMS reminder modules (unconfigured or single-channel); answering services (answer but don't follow up or report); review software $75–800/mo; front-desk staff (~€30k+, busy with in-person patients). All VERIFIED/INFERENCE per Section 2.
- **Weakness in current solutions:** *nobody owns the full chain* enquiry → answered → booked → reminded → attended → reviewed, and nobody reports it weekly as one number.
- **Outcome sold:** "More attended appointments from the enquiries you already pay for — measured weekly." Not marketing, not software: recovered revenue.
- **Manual concierge delivery (no build):** missed-call text-back (off-the-shelf: WhatsApp Business / OpenPhone-class tool or the clinic's existing number + a forwarding rule); Darren personally runs 5-minute follow-up on web/form/social enquiries during agreed hours; configures behavioural SMS reminder sequence (the Irish govt RCT wording is the template — VERIFIED it works) + deposit policy for high-value slots; post-visit review request flow; one-page Friday leak report (calls in, answered, recovered, booked, attended, reviews).
- **Minimum build:** none. Templates and a spreadsheet. Software only after ~5 paying clinics prove the workflow.
- **Paid pilot:** 30 days, **€990** including setup. Deliverables above. Success metric agreed in writing at kickoff (e.g. ≥10 recovered/attended appointments or ≥3x pilot fee in booked treatment value).
- **Continuation:** €495/mo (system + reporting + review engine) or €995/mo (+ Darren runs the follow-up desk + GBP/content from the existing SupaDesign menu). Matches live site tiers exactly.
- **Delivery hours:** setup ~8–10 hrs; steady-state ~3–5 hrs/clinic/month at €495, ~8–10 at €995. Gross margin ~85–90% (tool costs €30–60/clinic/mo). At 25–30 growth hours/week Darren can carry ~12–18 clinics solo.

- **First 100 prospects:** Cork city/county alone lists 60+ dental practices and 80+ physio/chiro clinics on Google Maps; Munster + Dublin takes the list past 400. Dental Council and CORU registers cross-check ownership names.

Any two of the three across most conversations = the leak is already plugged in this market; kill and move to 4B.
