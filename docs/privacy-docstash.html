DocStash — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 July 2026

DocStash is a document scanner and PDF tool. It's built to need as little of your data as possible — your documents are yours, and they stay on your device.

What the app accesses

  • Camera — DocStash never requests the camera permission directly. Scanning is handled entirely by Google's on-device Document Scanner component (part of Google Play services), which manages its own camera access and hands the finished page images back to DocStash.
  • Storage (app-private only) — scanned page images, the text recognized on them, and your document library are stored in DocStash's private app storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere by DocStash.
  • Internet — used only so Google Play services can deliver the on-device scanning component the first time it's needed, and so Google Play Billing can process an optional Pro purchase. DocStash does not operate any server of its own, and does not send your documents, scanned text, or images anywhere.

What we store

  • Your scanned documents, recognized text, and app settings are stored only on your device.
  • We do not require an account. We do not collect names, emails, contacts, or identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs, and we do not show ads.
  • Exported files (images or PDFs) are only created when you tap Export, and only shared with apps you explicitly choose from the share sheet.

On-device scanning and text recognition

Document scanning (edge detection, perspective correction) and text recognition (OCR) both run using Google ML Kit, processed on your device. DocStash does not send your photos or documents to any DocStash-operated server — there isn't one.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is a single one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. There is no subscription — Pro is paid once and stays unlocked.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.
