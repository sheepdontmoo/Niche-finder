Fairway Caddie — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 July 2026

Fairway Caddie is a golf GPS rangefinder and scorecard app. We built it to need as little of your data as possible.

What the app accesses

  • Location (GPS) — used only while the app is open, to calculate distances from where you are standing to the green and to find golf courses near you. Your location is processed on your device. It is not stored by us, not sent to our servers (we don't operate any), and not shared with anyone.
  • Internet — used only to download golf course maps from OpenStreetMap's public Overpass API when you tap "Find my course". The request contains approximate coordinates needed to find the course and nothing else.

What we store

  • Your scorecards, settings, and downloaded courses are stored only on your device.
  • We do not require an account. We do not collect names, emails, contacts, or identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs, and we do not show ads.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions (Caddie Plus) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.
