# CONTENT_PILLARS.md — content variety system for remote1stjobs.com

The problem this fixes: content drifting into one focus (e.g. "fake vs real remote jobs → sign up").
Every batch must vary across THREE axes — **pillar** (topic), **format**, and **intent**.
Rotate so no two consecutive posts share a pillar, and the sign-up CTA appears on ~1 in 10 posts, not every one.

## The 6 pillars (rotate evenly)
1. **REAL JOBS** — actual live remote roles / weekly spotlights across ALL categories (marketing, sales, design, CS, ops, finance, data, product, tech). The product itself.
2. **GET HIRED** — practical, genuinely useful how-to: spotting fake listings, remote CV/portfolio, async interviews, salary negotiation, EU/UK right-to-work, time-zone strategy.
3. **REMOTE LIFE** — aspirational / lifestyle: freedom, location independence, day-in-the-life, work-life design. Make people *want* it.
4. **REAL TALK** — relatable / community: the shared pains and wins, memes, "tag someone who…", poll questions. Make people feel seen and comment.
5. **THE DATA** — stats, hiring trends, RTO news, salary benchmarks, regional demand. Builds authority and gets shared. Must be real — web-search first.
6. **HOT TAKES** — Darren's opinion / myth-busting (incl. the fake-remote angle). Bold and provocative — but only ONE pillar of six, never the whole feed.

## Format variety (rotate; don't default to one square)
- **Single graphic** (1:1) — one big idea / headline.
- **Carousel** (multi-slide 1:1) — tips, listicles, how-tos, step-by-steps, job round-ups.
- **Stat / quote card** — one number or line, clean and shareable (THE DATA, REAL TALK).
- **Text / poll post** — no graphic or a simple one; a question or poll to drive comments (REAL TALK).
- **Job cards** — the glassy-card layout for REAL JOBS round-ups.

## Intent mix (per ~10 posts)
- **~60% VALUE** — educate / inspire / entertain. No hard CTA; end on a question.
- **~30% ENGAGEMENT** — community, polls, tag-a-friend, hot takes.
- **~10% CONVERSION** — explicit "sign up for free remote job alerts at remote1stjobs.com".
Never stack two hard-sell posts back to back.

## Rotation rules
- Track every post in CONTENT_PROGRESS.md: date, pillar, format, intent, topic.
- Never repeat a pillar two posts in a row; never repeat a specific topic.
- Each pillar pairs with its strongest format(s); vary the headline framework via `/headline` (MrBeast/Hormozi/Sabri) on top.
- All roles, UK/Europe/EMEA. Voice is Darren's. Every post ends on a question. Never start with "I".
