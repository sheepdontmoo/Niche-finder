# Cold outreach — lead with the sample, never the pitch

**Rule:** low volume, high fit, fully personalized. The human approves every send.
The goal of the first touch is *not* to sell — it's to give away a free 10-prospect
sample so quality does the selling. No bulk blasts, no fake urgency, no dishonest claims.

---

## Target segments (fastest to close first)

1. **B2B lead-gen / outbound agencies** — they resell leads; instant grasp of value, buy volume.
2. **Recruiters / staffing** — live and die by fresh, signal-rich contact lists.
3. **B2B SaaS with a small SDR/founder-led sales motion** — need pipeline, no data team.
4. **Consultancies / fractional execs** — need a steady trickle of qualified targets.

---

## Email — touch 1 (the sample offer)

**Subject:** 10 free {niche} leads — no catch

Hi {first_name},

I build enriched prospect lists for {segment} — verified contacts, each tagged with the
buying signal that makes them worth a message.

I'd like to build you a **free 10-prospect sample** for {their_ICP} so you can judge the
quality directly. If it's sharper than what you're working now, we can talk about a full
list. If not, you keep the 10 and we part friends.

Want me to build it? Just reply with the niche + titles you target.

{signature}

---

## Email — touch 2 (value nudge, +3 days, only if no reply)

**Subject:** re: 10 free {niche} leads

Hi {first_name}, quick follow-up — the sample offer still stands. To make it concrete,
here's the kind of signal I tag each contact with: {one_real_example_signal}. Reply with
your target niche and I'll have 10 back to you within a day.

{signature}

---

## Email — touch 3 (breakup, +5 days)

**Subject:** closing the loop

No worries if lead lists aren't a priority right now, {first_name} — I'll close this out.
If it becomes one, the free-sample offer is always open. Wishing you a strong quarter.

{signature}

## Guardrails
- Every send is human-approved. No automated bulk sending.
- Only claim what we can deliver. The free sample must be real and genuinely good.
- Honor opt-outs immediately. Respect privacy and platform terms.
- Personalize the {braces} — a template that still reads as a template gets deleted.
