## The lead horse: done-for-you AI product & brand visuals

We sell what small brands and sellers pay photographers and studios hundreds-to-thousands
for — but delivered in hours at ~1% of the cost, with the quality shown *before* payment.

**Credit economics:** a premium 2K image = **75 credits**. ~50k credits ≈ **660 premium
images**. A €59 order that delivers 10 images costs us ~750 credits (~€0 marginal, already
funded). Margin is effectively 100%.

### Two productized offers (both provable, ROI-obvious, fast)

**A. E-commerce product visuals & ads** *(primary — biggest buyer pool)*
Plain product photo in → scroll-stopping lifestyle scenes, clean studio shots, and ad
creatives out. Buyers: Shopify / Etsy / Amazon sellers, small DTC brands. They track
conversion, so better images = obvious ROI.

**B. Real-estate virtual staging** *(second — proven per-unit market)*
Empty room in → beautifully furnished room out. Buyers: agents, short-let hosts. Staged
listings sell faster; agents already pay €20–50/photo for this. Nano Banana Pro's
reference-guided editing nails it.

Blended ~€70 average order. **~360 orders over 90 days ≈ €25k** (≈4/day). Marketplaces
supply buyer traffic; retainers compress the count. Aggressive but structurally sound
because delivery is minutes and cost is already sunk.
