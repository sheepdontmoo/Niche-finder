# What's actually making money online now — ranked menu (researched 2026-07)

Research across Upwork/Fiverr demand data, Indie Hackers, Gumroad/Etsy income reports,
and creator-economy stats. Scored for OUR situation: two-person team (human handles
identity/payments/publishing; AI builds software + generates premium visuals/video via a
funded Magnific studio), **no audience, no brand, €0**, need **€25k collected in 90 days**.

Scoring 1–5 (5 = best). **Fit** = leverages our edge. **Dist.** = how easily we reach
buyers cold (5 = built-in traffic).

| # | Option | Speed | Ceiling | Low cost | Dist. | Fit | Notes |
|---|--------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|-------|
| 1 | **Productized AI services on Upwork/Fiverr** | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | Marketplace = built-in buyers, solves our #1 gap. AI-video editing **+329% YoY** client spend (fastest-growing category), AI integration +178%, chatbots +71%. $1–5k projects. |
| 2 | **Done-for-you AI automation / agent builds for SMBs** | 3 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 5 | $2.5–5k setup + $500–2k/mo retainer; **3–5 clients ≈ $10k/mo recurring**. We can actually *build* (real moat vs course-kids). Saturated with hype, so proof + niche win. |
| 3 | **High-ticket AI video ad pipeline for DTC brands** | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | Sell the *system/service* (€1–3k/mo), NOT $9 clips. AI UGC now 10–50× cheaper than creators; brands want volume. Our Magnific video + build edge fits perfectly. |
| 4 | **AI-built websites / landing pages for SMBs (Wix)** | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 | Productized €500–2k builds. We have Wix tools + can build. Local + cold outreach. Steady but delivery-heavy. |
| 5 | **Niche micro-SaaS / AI tool (build once)** | 2 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 5 | Marc Lou ~$1M/yr, Levels' Photo AI $132k/mo — but they had audiences. 30–60 day MVP is real; distribution is the killer. Great *compounding* bet, risky as the 90-day number. |
| 6 | **Digital products on Etsy/Gumroad** | 4 | 2 | 5 | 3 | 4 | Etsy has 4× Gumroad traffic. But Gumroad median **$72/mo, 44% earn $0**. First sale can come in hours; ceiling too low for €25k/90d without volume + a hit niche. |
| 7 | **AI consulting / audits (high-ticket)** | 3 | 4 | 5 | 2 | 3 | $80–300/hr, 1–2 clients moves the needle. But trust-gated — hard cold with no track record yet. |
| 8 | **Faceless AI YouTube/TikTok + affiliate** | 1 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 5 | **6–12 months** to monetize; months 1–3 are pre-revenue. Wrong tool for a 90-day sprint. Good long-game, not this race. |

## Avoid (commodity races / too slow for the sprint)
- Generic **AI product-photo gigs** at $5–25 — floor is racing to zero (my earlier idea; correctly rated 0/10).
- **$5–9 AI UGC videos** sold per-unit — same floor. Sell the *service/system*, not the unit.
- **Cold lead-list selling** — commoditized, GDPR-fraught, no moat.
- **Faceless content** as the primary 90-day play — too slow to first cash.

## The synthesis (why the top 3 combine)
Our fatal weakness in every past idea was **distribution** — no audience, cold brand.
**Marketplaces fix that**: Upwork/Fiverr bring high-intent buyers to us, and the exact
categories exploding there (AI video, automation, agent builds) are the ones we're *built*
to deliver. So:

**Top 3 for the 90-day €25k sprint:**
1. **Entry / fast cash → Upwork + Fiverr productized AI services** (video-ad editing +
   automation builds). Buyers already there; first orders in days, not months. This is how
   we get the first verified euros and the reviews that unlock everything else.
2. **High-ticket engine → AI automation / agent builds for SMBs.** Convert marketplace
   buyers + targeted cold outreach into €2.5–5k builds + retainers. This is what actually
   reaches €25k (few clients, recurring).
3. **Compounding bet → one niche micro-SaaS**, built in parallel cheaply. Doesn't have to
   carry the 90-day number; it raises the ceiling and outlives the sprint.

**One-line plan:** land fast paid work on marketplaces → use proof + retainers to close
high-ticket automation builds → spin a small SaaS on the side. All delivery uses tools we
already have; every live/publish/payment step stops at a red light for the human.
