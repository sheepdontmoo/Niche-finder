# The Millionaire-Brain Filter — our decision system

Built from the people who actually did it. Every opportunity must pass this filter or it
gets killed — no matter how "nice" it sounds. This replaces freelancer logic (sell time)
with owner logic (build assets + leverage).

## The role models & their one rule each
- **Naval Ravikant** — *Own equity, not time.* Wealth = assets that earn while you sleep.
  **Code + media are permissionless leverage** — they work for you at zero marginal cost.
  *(These two are exactly our superpowers: we build software and generate infinite media.)*
- **Alex Hormozi** — *Pick a starving crowd before the offer:* **Pain × Purchasing power ×
  Easy-to-target × Growing.** Front-end must repay acquisition cost within **30 days**.
  *"The only thing that beats free is fast."*
- **Pieter Levels / Marc Lou** — *Ship fast, launch cheap, revenue is the only validation.*
  Portfolio of bets. **Build in public** for distribution. ($3.1M/yr and $141k/mo MRR, solo.)
- **Indie-hacker consensus** — the money is in **boring, high-trust niches with painful,
  expensive, recurring problems** (legal, compliance, real estate, recruiting, construction,
  logistics, healthcare). *"Your generic AI product is not a real business."*

## The 8 gates (2 are pass/fail, 6 are scored 1–5)

| # | Gate | Rule | Type |
|---|------|------|------|
| 1 | **Own the asset** | Builds equity that earns while we sleep — NOT time-for-money | pass/fail |
| 2 | **Code + media leverage** | Scales at ~zero marginal cost via software + content | pass/fail |
| 3 | **Starving crowd** | Pain × Purchasing power × Easy-to-target × Growing | 1–5 |
| 4 | **Grand Slam Offer** | Dream outcome, high certainty, fast, low effort — dumb to say no | 1–5 |
| 5 | **30-day cash** | Front-end repays CAC in 30 days; ideally **pre-sell before building** | 1–5 |
| 6 | **Ship fast & cheap** | Paying v1 in days–weeks; revenue = validation | 1–5 |
| 7 | **Owned distribution** | We manufacture traffic (programmatic SEO + free-tool wedge + our content engine) — not rent it | 1–5 |
| 8 | **Ceiling compounds** | Clear path past €25k to 7 figures | 1–5 |

| Opportunity | G1 | G2 | Starve | Offer | Cash | Ship | Dist | Ceiling | Verdict |

| Freelance/agency gigs (Upwork/Fiverr) | ❌ | ❌ | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 1 | **KILL as core** — time-for-money, no equity. Only useful as short-term fuel. |

| **Vertical AI SaaS for a painful high-trust niche** | ✅ | ✅ | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | **★ WINNER** — owned, scalable, starving B2B crowd, pre-sellable, 7-fig ceiling. |

| Digital products / templates | ~ | ✅ | 2 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 2 | 2 | **Weak** — low pain, low ceiling, distribution-starved. |
| Faceless AI content | ❌ | ✅ | 3 | 3 | 1 | 4 | 3 | 4 | **KILL for 90 days** — 6–12mo to cash; not an owned product. |

## The verdict (what survives the filter)
**Build one vertical AI SaaS that solves a painful, expensive, recurring workflow for a
starving B2B crowd — distributed through a programmatic-SEO + free-tool wedge, and
PRE-SOLD for cash before it's fully built.**

- Hits both pass/fail gates (owned asset + code/media leverage).
- €25k in 90 days = **pre-sales + early subscriptions** (e.g. 50 lifetime deals @ €500, or
  ~250 subs @ €40/mo). Cash arrives in weeks via pre-sell, not months.
- Ceiling is 7 figures — real indie examples hit **$1M ARR** serving exactly these niches.
- Distribution is **owned** (we generate the SEO pages and the content), not rented.

## The one decision left: WHICH starving crowd
This is the next research pass — score candidate painful niches on Pain × Purchasing power
× Easy-to-target × Growing, then pick the beachhead and pre-sell it. Candidate niches from
the research: **legal/paralegal ops, compliance, real-estate marketing, recruiting/staffing,
construction/trades admin, logistics, senior care, insurance.** Pick by starving-crowd
score + how directly our media+code edge is the magic.
