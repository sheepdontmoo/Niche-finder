Privacy Policy — Estimated Delivery Date

Last updated: 11 July 2026

Estimated Delivery Date (the "App") is a Shopify app that displays an estimated delivery date on a merchant's storefront. This policy explains what the App accesses and stores.

What the App stores

  • Merchant delivery settings. The configuration you enter (processing time, cutoff hour, shipping window, working days, wording, date format, locale) is stored as a metafield on your Shopify shop.
  • Authentication session. A standard Shopify app session (shop domain and access token) so the App can function. This is stored securely and used only to operate the App.

What the App does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or process any customer personal data (no names, emails, addresses, or order contents).
  • The delivery estimate shown to shoppers is calculated in the shopper's own browser; no shopper data is sent to us.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Compliance requests

Because the App holds no customer personal data, GDPR/CCPA customers/data_request and customers/redact requests have no customer data to return or erase. On app uninstall (shop/redact), the App's stored session and settings are removed.

Data retention

Settings and session data are retained while the App is installed and removed when you uninstall the App.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact the app support address listed on the Shopify App Store listing.
