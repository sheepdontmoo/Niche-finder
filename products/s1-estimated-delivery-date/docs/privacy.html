Privacy Policy — SupaDatewise

Last updated: 30 August 2026

SupaDatewise (the "App") is a Shopify app that displays an estimated delivery date on a merchant's storefront. This policy explains what the App accesses and stores.

What the App stores

  • Merchant delivery settings. The configuration you enter (processing time, cutoff hour, shipping window, working days, wording, date format, locale) is stored as a metafield on your Shopify shop.
  • Authentication session. A standard Shopify app session (shop domain and access token) so the App can function. This is stored securely and used only to operate the App.

What the App does not do

  • It does not request or store shopper names, emails, addresses, or order contents.
  • The delivery estimate is calculated in the shopper's browser. Before revealing it, the block makes a Shopify-signed app-proxy request to confirm the merchant's active subscription. Shopify may include a logged-in customer identifier and request metadata; the App ignores that identifier and does not persist it.
  • It does not sell shopper data. Shopify processes the signed proxy and subscription check as the App's platform provider.

Compliance requests

Because the App does not persist customer records, GDPR/CCPA customers/data_request and customers/redact requests have no stored customer record to return or erase. On app uninstall, the App deletes its stored Shopify app session. Merchant delivery settings are app-owned Shopify metafields, whose post-uninstall lifecycle is controlled by Shopify.

Data retention

The App deletes its stored session when you uninstall. Shopify deletes the app-owned metafield definitions but may temporarily retain metafields and their values without definitions; a quick reinstall may reassociate those retained values. See Shopify's metafield-definition documentation for the platform lifecycle.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact the app support address listed on the Shopify App Store listing.
